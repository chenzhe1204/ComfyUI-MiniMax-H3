# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements with evidence.
- Respect the MiniMax H3 Community License and its territorial restrictions.
- Do not use the project to generate or distribute content that violates the
  model's Acceptable Use Policy or applicable law.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private
information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate
this code. Reports can be sent to the maintainer contact listed in the repository
profile. Reports will be handled confidentially.
